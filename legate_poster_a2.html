What Legate Does For You

Encrypted Capsules

Write rich messages and attach photos or video. Each capsule is sealed with independent, end-to-end encryption before it ever leaves your device.

Automatic Check-Ins

Choose how often we check on you — every 7, 14, 30, 60 days, or a custom interval. Miss it, and delivery begins on its own.

Trusted Beneficiaries

Assign the people who should receive your legacy. They get your instructions, never your account access or settings.

Grace Period Safety Net

A configurable buffer of 3 to 30 days sits between a missed check-in and delivery — protection against false alarms, never against inaction.

Full Activity Log

Every check-in, edit, and delivery is recorded. Review your vault's history any time from the Activity tab.

Independent Encryption

Create unlimited capsules for different people. Each is encrypted on its own, so access is never all-or-nothing.
